# Audience profile

## Truths about the audience
- The primary audience is recruiters and employers evaluating front-end talent.
- The audience values clarity, polish, and professionalism as much as creativity.
- They are likely to respond to experiences that feel immersive and memorable without being confusing.
- The audience is not expecting a full production product; they are expecting a compelling demonstration of skill.
- The project should communicate both technical competence and creative taste.
- The experience should feel approachable, atmospheric, and emotionally resonant.

## What the audience needs
- A clear understanding of the interaction without reading a manual.
- An experience that demonstrates strong visual direction.
- A result that feels thoughtful, premium, and intentional.
- Front-end proof that the creator can combine motion, design, and interaction into one cohesive experience.
- A project that reads as reasonably approachable and understandable, even if it is conceptually creative.
