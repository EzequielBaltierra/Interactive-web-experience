# Creator style

## Design truths
- The project should feel dark, atmospheric, and quietly mystical.
- The aesthetic should be elegant, soft, and premium rather than playful or cartoonish.
- Visual language should prioritize mood, contrast, and focus.
- The experience should feel like a curated art direction, not a generic template.
- The design should support the narrative experience without overpowering it.
- Design direction is not fully finalized yet; do not overcommit to a style system before the visual direction is confirmed.

## Interaction truths
- The experience should reward attention and curiosity.
- The flower interaction should feel ritualistic and meaningful.
- Motion and transitions should be smooth, intentional, and minimal.
- The selected flower should become the clear focal point while the rest of the scene supports the atmosphere.
- The final reveal should feel like a conclusion, not an abrupt technical state change.

## Quality bar
- Elegant over busy.
- Cohesive over fragmented.
- Memorable over generic.
- Calm confidence over noise.
- Minimal change over broad redesign while the design direction is still being settled.
