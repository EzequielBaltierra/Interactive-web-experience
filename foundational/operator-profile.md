# Operator profile

## Truths about the developer
- I am building this as a portfolio-focused front-end project.
- I want the experience to demonstrate strong creative and technical judgment.
- I value clarity, polish, and intentional design over novelty for novelty’s sake.
- I prefer front-end-only solutions unless a feature clearly requires backend support.
- I want the project to feel elegant and immersive, not noisy or overbuilt.
- I want implementation choices to support a clean demo story for recruiters and future collaborators.
- I care about maintainability and readability in code, especially in a project meant to be showcased.

## Working preferences
- Keep solutions simple and understandable.
- Favor minimal invasion and minimal refactors over broad rewrites.
- Prefer readable beginner-friendly code over clever abstractions.
- Favor polished UX and minimal friction.
- A good result is one that looks intentional and feels coherent.
- Avoid unnecessary complexity or feature creep.
- Do not over-invest in design decisions that are still undecided; keep the project flexible until the final direction is set.
- Prefer incremental, reversible changes that preserve working behavior.
